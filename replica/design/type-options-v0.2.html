Second pass on type. Twenty-four display faces on the wall, eight full pairings, and the numerals on their own.

The mark, the palette, the state language and the five mocks are approved and fixed. The only thing being chosen here is the display face (wordmark, page titles, big numbers) and, optionally, the interface face and the data face underneath it. Every sample is the same console fragment, so only the type changes.

F · Decide

Pick a pairing by number, or compose one: a display face from the wall, an interface face from D, a data face from E. Mixing is fine; they are independent tokens.

My pick
T1 Mission control (Michroma) for the product, with T3's Doto numerals borrowed for the KPI tiles and the age ring only.
Michroma gives the wordmark and titles a real lineage (the control-room look that predates every game UI) and ages well; Doto on the big numbers alone adds the readout character you asked for without making a single table harder to read. Interface stays IBM Plex Sans, data stays IBM Plex Mono, so nothing else in the mocks moves.
Runner-up
T4 Engineer's terminal (Kode Mono + Geist)
If you want the whole console to feel like a well-made terminal rather than a control room. Cleanest at small sizes of the character faces.
Boldest
T3 Instrument readout all the way, Doto on the wordmark too.
Unmistakable, nothing else in the category looks like it; the tray icon and the favicon still carry the M1 mark, so the dot-matrix wordmark only ever appears large.
Not recommended
T7 Orbitron (genre cliché), Zen Dots / Audiowide / Syncopate (display-only faces that fail at 12 px), Iceland / Iceberg (fun names, dated shapes).

a T number, or "display X + UI Y + data Z", and whether the Doto-numerals borrow is in or out.
