Purge {{.Name}} permanently? Purge is one-way.

  • Deletes this device's backups from storage, and its escrowed repository key with them. They cannot be recovered or restored afterwards, by anyone.
  • A storage provider with versioning, object lock or a minimum storage duration may keep, and bill, the deleted data until its own window ends. The console cannot shorten that.
  • An agent still installed on the machine stays REFUSED: uninstall it there.
  • Starts at once and cannot be cancelled. The device shows as purging until storage reports its repository gone; then its record is deleted. Its audit rows stay.
